# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

The primary audience is research mentors, technical collaborators, internship reviewers, and engineering peers who need to understand Zhuang Chengbo's direction and verify representative work quickly.

## Product Purpose

The site is an evidence-backed personal portfolio for simulation systems, digital twins, robotics, semantic 3D modeling, and practical AI. It should establish a coherent technical direction, surface running systems and public records, and provide a direct route to collaboration without becoming a full resume archive.

## Brand Personality

Precise, exploratory, and grounded. The voice is confident without overclaiming, technically specific without becoming inaccessible, and personal without reading like a travel diary.

## Anti-references

- Long resume pages that list every course, award, and assignment with equal weight.
- Generic AI landing pages built from gradients, decorative cards, and vague claims.
- Portfolio copy that implies institutional affiliation, publication status, or research validation beyond the available evidence.
- Overly formal academic pages that hide the person behind dense credentials.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with the person and the direction, then prove both with representative systems.
2. Prefer deployed products, reproducible repositories, and formal records over broad claims.
3. Keep the simulation, digital twin, robotics, and applied AI narrative visible across sections.
4. Maintain accurate dates, project levels, ownership, and application status.
5. Preserve a concise homepage; detailed academic history remains in the private CV.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA contrast and keyboard access. Preserve semantic landmarks, visible focus states, reduced-motion behavior, meaningful image alternatives, and layouts that remain readable on narrow mobile screens and at browser zoom.
